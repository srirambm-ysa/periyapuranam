Chapter 01 — Manikkavachagar (Vadhavurar) — A Note

Manikkavachagar stands outside the Periyapuranam's sixty-three Nayanmars and nine Thogai Adiyars; living after Sundarar's age, his life finds no place in Sekkizhar's Thiruthondar Puranam. Yet with Appar, Sundarar and Sambandar he is honoured as one of the four Samayacharyas, the Nalvar.

Vadhavurar, minister to the Pandyan king, was taken into grace by the Lord at Tirupperundurai and became Manikkavachagar. His Thiruvasagam and Thirukkovaiyar shine as the eighth Thirumurai; the proverb says that one who does not melt for the Thiruvasagam will melt for nothing uttered.
